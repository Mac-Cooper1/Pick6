# Pick 6 — Official League Rules (2026)

## The Draft

Each player drafts **5 teams — one from each conference slot**:

- **SEC**
- **Big Ten**
- **ACC + Notre Dame**
- **Big 12**
- **Group of 6** — AAC, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Sun Belt, and the rebuilt Pac-12

No two players in a league can roster the same team. The draft is a **live snake draft** (5 rounds) in the Draft tab — the commissioner schedules it, everyone joins the draft room, and each pick is on a clock (autopick uses your queue, then best available by AP rank). Results live in the **Draft Recap** tab.

## Scoring (per team, per week)

| Result | Points |
| :---- | :---- |
| Win | +1 |
| Win as underdog of **+3.5 or more** | +2 |
| Loss | 0 |
| Loss as favorite of **−3.5 or more** | −1 |

Favorite/underdog comes from the pre-game betting line. Spreads smaller than 3.5 (and pick'ems) score as regular wins/losses, and a game with no posted line scores as a regular result. Postponed or cancelled games score 0.

Each team scores **exactly one** of the four outcomes per week — the values don't stack. An underdog win is worth 2 points total (not 1&nbsp;+&nbsp;2).

## The Season

The season runs on ESPN's official calendar: 2026 is **weeks 1–13**, from the opening games of Aug 22–29 through rivalry weekend, ending Saturday, Nov 28. (There is no "Week 0" — the late-August openers are part of Week 1, which runs through Labor Day.)

**Nothing after week 13 counts**: not the conference championship games (Dec 4–5), not Army-Navy (Dec 12), not the bowls or the College Football Playoff. Everyone's last game is on the same weekend, and the leaderboard is final once it's scored. Army and Navy play each other after the season ends, so they are scored on 11 games; nearly every other team plays 12. A game postponed past week 13 doesn't count either.

**Every game your team plays counts.** Because ESPN's Week 1 covers two weekends, some teams play twice in it. A team's first game counts as its Week 1 game; its second game counts as its **Week 2 game if the team is off in Week 2** (Florida State: Aug 29 = Week 1, Sep 7 vs SMU = Week 2). If the team also plays in Week 2 (UNLV played Aug 30, Sep 6 and Sep 12), both early games count in Week 1. Season totals are the same either way; only the column changes.

## Standings

One shared leaderboard, cumulative all season. No head-to-head matchups — just points stacking up. Check the **Standings** tab for the current week and overall race.

**Tiebreaker: tougher schedule.** Players tied on points are ordered by the combined **strength of schedule** of their five teams: add up each team's SOS rank from ESPN's FPI ([the SOS column here](https://www.espn.com/college-football/fpi/_/view/resume): 1 is the hardest schedule in FBS, 138 the easiest), and the **lower total ranks higher**. SOS ranks update daily with the scores.

## Week 6 Swap

Every player gets **one swap**: drop a team, add an unowned team from the **same conference slot**. All swaps happen at once, before any week 6 game:

- **Week 5 (from Sep 28, 2026): set your list.** On the **Week 6 Swap** tab, every unowned team is listed by the Pick 6 points it has scored this season; tap Add to put one on your list against your team in the same slot. Rank up to 10 swaps in priority order (for example: 1. South Carolina → Florida, 2. Michigan → Iowa, 3. Army → Navy). Your list is private, and you can change it as often as you like until it locks.
- **Start of week 6 (Mon Oct 5, 3:00 AM ET): lists lock and the swap runs** for every league in reverse standings through week 5: worst record first, ties broken by the tiebreaker above (the easier combined schedule goes first). When your turn comes you get the **highest swap on your list that's still possible**; the rest of your list is ignored. If the player ahead of you took Florida, you get your next choice (Iowa, for your Big Ten team).
- **Only teams nobody owns when lists lock can be added.** A team dropped in the swap can't be picked up by anyone, so a better record never inherits what a worse one let go.
- No list, or nothing on it still possible: you keep your five.

Points already scored stay exactly as they were: your old team keeps weeks 1–5, and your new team counts from week 6 on.

## Playing

Create an account (email + password), join the league with its **join code**, and you're in:

- **Standings** — where everyone stands, week by week and overall
- **League** — everyone's teams with upcoming games and lines
- **Draft / Draft Recap** — the live draft room and the full results
- **Week 6 Swap** — every unowned team by Pick 6 points, your swap list (week 5), then how everyone's swap went

Scores update automatically on a schedule (and the commissioner can sync on demand).
